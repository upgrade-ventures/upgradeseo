- Say your product is "built on UpgradeSEO" or "a fork of UpgradeSEO".
- Use the name to refer to this project in writing, talks, and comparisons.
- Keep the name and branding while you run your own instance for yourself, your
  company, or your clients.
- Contribute back under the same name.

## Please do not, without written permission

- Publish a modified version under the name "UpgradeSEO", or a name close
  enough to be mistaken for it.
- Use our logo as the icon of your product.
- Imply that we endorse, support, or maintain your fork.
- Register the name or a confusable variant as a domain, package, app-store
  listing, or company name.

If you distribute a modified version, rename it. Everything else in the project
is yours to take.

## Why this exists rather than an attribution clause

We considered a badgeware licence that would require our name in your footer.
Two reasons we did not:

Most of this code descends from an MIT-licensed project, so an attribution
clause would be unenforceable over the majority of the files, and anyone who
objected could simply fork our upstream instead. And badgeware licences are
banned outright inside many companies, which would cost the project the
adoption that makes it worth maintaining.

MIT plus a trademark policy is the same arrangement Rust, Docker and WordPress
use. The code stays free. The name stays ours.
